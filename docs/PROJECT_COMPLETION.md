# Project completion review

The purpose is a reliable matching-policy and human/AV allocation research
simulator, built on FleetPy. Useful additions and their evidence remain intact.
This review does not create another numbered phase.

## Acceptance checklist

- Trace every original proposed mechanism to code, tests and scoped evidence.
- Keep legacy/default matching behavior and protected references unchanged.
- Provide an executable, documented multi-policy comparison workflow.
- Expose KPI changes with units, denominators, clocks, uncertainty and policy
  parameter differences; keep served-only selection and missing values visible.
- Verify actual AV types/allocation, eligibility and physical movement rather
  than infer AV use from a policy label.
- Reject changed raw inputs, incompatible source/assumption strata, unmatched
  seeds and fake replay replications.
- Preserve raw/source/config/runtime/output records, failures/fixes and actual
  publication identity as Xiaoyu Su.
- Identify data/calibration limitations without inventing field observations.

[Original proposal reconciliation](PROPOSAL_AUDIT.md),
[comparison workflow](POLICY_COMPARISON.md) and [KPI contract](KPI_GUIDE.md)
provide the user-facing entry points. The older phase guides remain engineering
history and specialized study documentation.

## Verified completion

The scoped proposal/software checklist is complete for research use. Validation
records **485 passing tests**, zero failures/errors/skips, **509 complete
regression simulations**, **32 restored windows**, and **68 focused simulations**.
All441 prior full cases retain4124 checked output
files byte for byte;128 window tables retain exact values. Matching/AV policy
algorithms and protected historical evidence are unchanged.

The new workflow demonstrates **57 fresh physical runs**, **42 unique conditions**
and15 exact reference replays across three matching controllers and three cities.
Independent raw-event/KPI checks pass. Historical allocation/repositioning reports
reanalyze72 existing records, retaining their original simulation sources.

The project now has one documented multi-policy entry point,46 explicit KPIs,
paired movements/uncertainty, actual AV allocation and human activity metrics,
immutable raw/config/spec snapshots, parameter-difference disclosure and strict
input/source grouping. Fresh examples show both improvements and adverse effects;
there is no automatic winning policy. The [measured results](validation/project_completion/RESULTS.md)
and [complete evidence](validation/project_completion/README.md) contain exact
counts, commands, failures/fixes and source/input/runtime/output records.

## Empirical boundaries remain explicit

Public completed trips do not identify online supply, rejected/unseen requests,
driver refusal coefficients, AV adoption or actual cancellation behavior.
Scenario assumptions and residual calibration errors remain visible. Dynamic
rider adoption/choice was a limitation in the brief, not an unfinished promised
mechanism. Short evaluation windows are not whole operating days. These data
limits prevent a claim of field-calibrated performance; they do not prevent
matched conditional policy experiments.

Useful completed robustness/mechanism studies are retained. No further numbered
phase is required to satisfy this scoped completion checklist.
